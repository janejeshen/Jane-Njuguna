Award: 2nd Place - Top Female

- Built an optimized LightGBM ensemble pipeline with Optuna hyperparameter tuning.
- Applied GroupKFold cross-validation to prevent spatial leakage.
- Achieved a Macro F1 score of 0.8663 in a competitive geospatial AI challenge.
